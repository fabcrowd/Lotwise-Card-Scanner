By default the scanner reads a screenshot of the page you scan. If you allow it to read photos on the sites you scan, it can use the full-size picture instead, which is more accurate. Photos are fetched without your cookies and analyzed on this device.
Help improve Sweep
Help improve recognition for everyone
If you turn this on, small pictures of the cards you correct or confirm, and the card you chose, are sent to help improve recognition for everyone. Small pictures of boxes you draw, remove or put back are sent too, and a removed box may show something that is not a card. The pictures become public and cannot be taken back. No page address, no full photo and no account is sent. It is off until you turn it on, and turning it off deletes your random install id and anything still waiting to be sent.

Advanced
Look-alike cards
A few cards look like many others to the scanner and win matches they should not. This steers matches away from them. It is off until its accuracy has been confirmed on a separate test set.
Straighten cards
Finds the four corners of each card and flattens it upright before it is read, which helps with tilted, sideways or loosely boxed cards. It is off until it has been confirmed to help on a separate test set.
